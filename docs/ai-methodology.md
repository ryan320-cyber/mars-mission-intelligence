# AI Methodology & Grounding Architecture

Architecture and anti-hallucination guardrails for the **Mars Grounded AI Scientist**.

## 1. Context Generation & Structured Telemetry Injection
The AI Science Assistant does not operate as an unconstrained chatbot. Every query is bound to structured telemetry context extracted dynamically from the backend analytical pipeline:
- Active mission and landing coordinates (Gale Crater, Elysium Planitia)
- Calibrated summary statistics (mean, median, IQR, 5th/95th percentiles)
- Top 5 multivariate anomalies identified by Isolation Forest
- Current Martian Environmental Challenge Index (MECI) and factor breakdown
- PDS dataset collection URNs and DOIs

---

## 2. AI Answer Contract (Directive 38)
Every response generated by the Mars AI Scientist must strictly adhere to the 5-field Answer Contract:

```text
[FINDING]
Direct empirical answer to the user's query.

[DATA EVIDENCE]
Actual numerical values, Sols, p-values, and statistics from the loaded telemetry.

[ANALYTICAL INTERPRETATION]
Planetary boundary layer physics and statistical inferences.

[LIMITATIONS]
Uncertainty estimates, missing sensor periods, or spatial field-of-view limits.

[SOURCE]
Authoritative NASA PDS dataset identifier, mission name, and instrument.
```

---

## 3. Anti-Hallucination Guardrails & Refusal of False Certainty (Directive 40)
The system strictly enforces refusal criteria:
1. **Unmeasured Variables**: If a query asks for variables not present in verified meteorological telemetry (e.g. subsurface oceans, methane plumes, human life-support metrics), the engine responds:
   > *"The requested subject is outside the scope of verified meteorological telemetry. No calibrated sensor telemetry exists in MSL REMS, InSight TWINS, or MRO MCS data products."*
2. **Data Sufficiency Meter (Directive 62)**: Every response outputs a verified data sufficiency rating:
   - `HIGH`: Over 100 continuous observations and validated uncertainty parameters.
   - `MODERATE`: Minor sensor gaps present; relative trends valid.
   - `LOW`: High missingness or localized uncalibrated interval.
   - `INSUFFICIENT`: Variable or temporal range absent.

---

## 4. Signature Wow Moment #2: Data → AI Traceability (Directive 61)
The AI response generates clickable evidence trace links (e.g. `[View Anomaly Timeline (Sol 2082)]`, `[Inspect MECI Factor Decomposition]`, `[Open Model Lab Residual Analysis]`).
When clicked, these links immediately scroll and navigate the user interface directly to the underlying raw chart and telemetry points, creating inspectable accountability between AI claims and NASA empirical data.
